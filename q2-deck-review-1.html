The First Engagement · The diagnostic

We arrive with questions, not a pitch.

The Constraint Map℠ diagnostic is three questions, asked in sequence. Each one makes a gap visible in the act of trying to answer it. The executive does not get told they have a problem. They experience it.

Question 1 · The Operational Graph
“Draw me the path from a customer interaction to its impact on your earnings call.”
They can describe the start and the end. They cannot draw the middle. The hand-wave across functions is the finding: no one owns the full thread.
Question 2 · The Misdiagnosis
“When your last big initiative missed, what was the root-cause diagnosis?”
The answer is always execution: people, change management, communication. The real cause was an architecture that could not have produced the outcome, and the tools to see that did not exist.
Question 3 · Measurement Sovereignty
“Who can tell you the customer-level cost of a one-point gain in retention?”
The number no one can produce is the proof. The architectural gap is not abstract. It reaches directly into the financial architecture, and it has a cost.
The mechanic
Spatial, then narrative, then numerical. By question three, the executive has experienced the gap, reinterpreted their past through it, and seen its financial footprint. The questions do the selling.

One weak domain caps the whole.

Readiness multiplies, it does not average. Four domains at “pretty good” do not add up to pretty good. A single weak domain craters the composite for all four.
