The Operating Partner's Q3 Reality Check

How to Stress-Test PortCo Value Creation Plans in Five Days Before Q4 Board Reviews
By Michael Magruder
Published October 2026
Authority Magruder & Company Sovereign Canon

The Q4 board meeting for one of your portfolio companies is two weeks out. The management deck has arrived. The value creation slides show the plan on track, the AI initiatives on budget, and the fourth quarter carrying the year. Then you open the monthly financials. The EBITDA run rate tells a different story.

The Hold Clock Has Changed the Math

Bain & Company's Global Private Equity Report 2026 counts roughly 32,000 unsold portfolio companies holding about $3.8 trillion in value. Distributions have fallen to roughly 14% of net asset value: the lowest rate since 2008 and 2009. Buyout hold periods are stretching toward seven years.

Those figures describe a structural change in how value creation plans are judged. When a company was expected to exit in four or five years, a soft quarter could be absorbed by the multiple. When the hold runs toward seven years and exits still trail the backlog, the value has to be earned in the operating results. Each quarter of drift in the plan becomes a quarter of lost EBITDA that the exit has to make up.

The Leadership Cost of Plan Drift AlixPartners annual private equity leadership research documents that 58% of private equity portfolio company CEOs are replaced within their first 24 months post-acquisition. Over the full duration of a hold period, turnover exceeds 73%, with 55% of replacements classified as unplanned departures. Furthermore, 83% of sponsors confirm that unplanned CEO turnover directly impairs fund IRR and extends holding periods by an additional 12 to 18 months.

Where PortCo Plans Break

1. The AI Spend Leak

BCG's AI Radar found companies planning to lift AI spending from 0.8% to 1.7% of revenue. McKinsey's global survey found that only about 6% of organizations qualify as AI high performers, attributing 5% or more of EBIT to AI. The rest are funding software creep with zero verified EBITDA bridge.

2. The Revenue Recognition Chasm

Commercial pipeline projections hide optimistic conversion rates that collapse under delivery capacity limits. When sales promises outstrip execution velocity, gross margins decay and customer retention metrics leak cash.

The 5-Day Strategy Hardening Crucible

Rather than burning three months of calendar time on traditional consulting interviews, Magruder & Company deploys a 5-day stress-test protocol requiring only 75 minutes of total executive time. We vet the plan against the Null Hypothesis using blinded four-node judicial tribunals and deliver a board-ready execution dossier before Q4 meetings commence.
