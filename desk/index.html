Hardening

Hardening something that's almost ready

Anything I complete or improve for you can be hardened, and so can your own work, whether it's nearly finished or about to be presented.

Hardening means I take it apart and put it through a tougher review than it will face in the room. I use the platforms and tools I have built to decompose it from an adversarial perspective, going further than a skeptical board, investor, or team would. You see exactly how deep the review went and what it found. Then we rebuild it together, quickly, adding data or market references where they strengthen your position. I don't just break it, I rebuild it. I'm glad to show you how it works.

You also know how accurate it is. Every hardened deliverable comes with reports that document what was checked, what was found, and what changed. That's very different from dropping something into AI and working with it as it drifts and hallucinates, often in ways you don't notice until it's too late and difficult to unwind. I also make sure nothing in it reads as if AI wrote it.

Depending on your audience, I prepare the questions and challenges you are likely to face, with answers ready, so you walk in prepared.
