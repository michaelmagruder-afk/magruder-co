Pressure-Testing & Hardening

Hardening an AI plan or board deck means pressure-testing every assumption and rebuilding weak sections

Every plan we build from the ground up is hardened before delivery, and if your team has already drafted an AI strategy, board update, or vendor investment case, I can pressure-test and harden that draft before you walk into the room.

Hardening means I take the draft apart and put it through a tougher adversarial review than it will face from your board, PE sponsor, CFO, or general counsel. Using the multi-model governance systems I have built over 190+ working sessions, I test every technical claim, cost assumption, adoption timeline, and risk mitigation against current institutional research (McKinsey, BCG, Gartner, HBR, MIT) and real-world enterprise failure modes.

I do not just break the work down; I rebuild the narrative and operating logic with you quickly so it is airtight. Every hardened AI plan comes with a verification report documenting what was checked, what was challenged, and what changed, along with a board Q&A preparation brief covering the hardest questions you will face and the strongest answers to each.

Every engagement runs under strict confidentiality boundaries: your company materials remain strictly private, never train public models, and read as clear, authoritative human prose rather than AI-generated text.
