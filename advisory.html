Core Executive Mandate

The Four Nondelegable Decision Gates

What separates the CEO from functional executives? Functional leaders optimize within domains. The CEO arbitrates across domains when the enterprise cannot maximize all objectives simultaneously. Across the late Q3 and Q4 governance corridor, these four gates require definitive executive judgment:

Gate 01
Govern the Enterprise AI Transition
Lead: The Executive Governed AI Manifest. Board and Audit committees require direct clarity on AI risk, IP boundaries, agent liabilities, and return thresholds before fall review cycles.
Gate 02
Lock the 2027 Strategic Bets
The CFO models budget scenarios; only the CEO arbitrates competing functional claims and decides what gets funded, starved, or stopped before the 2027 operating plan hardens.
Gate 03
Defend the Value Creation Narrative
Year-end commitments represent the CEO's personal credibility with directors, PE sponsors, and investors. Decide whether to defend the target or proactively reset expectations.
Gate 04
Resolve Leadership & Execution Authority
The 2027 plan will be built around the team in place today. Determine whether current leadership capability, operating model, and decision rights can execute the committed strategy.
