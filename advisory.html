Questions

Do hours expire?

Hours are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. Any unused hours expire at that point.

Can I purchase additional hours once I’ve used my block?

Yes — you can return to this link at any time to purchase another block at the applicable rate.

What’s the refund policy?

Unused hours may be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Hours that have been scheduled or completed are non-refundable.

How do I book sessions?

After payment, you’ll receive a confirmation email with a scheduling link. Use it to pick times that work for you.

Why do sessions include two practitioners?

Every advisory session is staffed by two Magruder & Company practitioners, so you get paired expertise and continuity of coverage in every block.

What is the Readiness Assessment?

The Readiness Assessment is a structured two-week engagement that maps your organization’s specific AI constraints using the Constraint Map™ diagnostic. It produces a scoping memo that defines exactly what the follow-on engagement covers and what it costs. The $7,500 deposit secures your slot and credits against the total fee.

What if I’m booking both the Readiness Assessment and the Enterprise Performance Corpus?

Select quantity 2 on the deposit link. Each engagement is deposited and priced separately, $7,500 per engagement, credited against that engagement’s own total fee.
