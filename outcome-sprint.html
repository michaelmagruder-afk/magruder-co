The Premier Common Asset: The Executive Governed AI Manifest

The most persistent challenge facing leadership teams right now is establishing an overarching framework for governed artificial intelligence that the entire enterprise can understand and sign off on.

The Executive Governed AI Manifest is not a dense technical manual. It is an authoritative executive instrument that establishes:

  • Explicit corporate boundary lines defining where AI systems may and may not operate.
  • Data protection and classification tiers safeguarding proprietary enterprise assets.
  • Mandatory human-in-the-loop decision gates for financial, legal, and operational actions.
  • A unified governance baseline that business unit leaders can immediately attach departmental policies to.
